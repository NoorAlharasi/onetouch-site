Findings

What the data says about the two questions this pilot exists to answer. Thresholds are stated so you can disagree with them.

Question 1 · Does the signal flow?

Will residents report through OneTouch rather than the old channels?

Question 2 · Does the system work?

Once a request is in, is it handled, recorded honestly and closed properly?

How to read this

📌
These thresholds are hypotheses, not laws. If one turns out to be wrong for this community, change it and say so in the write-up — that is a finding in itself.
⚠️
Under 15 real requests, no conclusion is drawn. Small samples produce confident-looking nonsense.
